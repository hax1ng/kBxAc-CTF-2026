## Instructions
In this crossword, each alphabetical character has been replaced by a randomly assigned number between 1 and 26 (inclusive).

The mapping between numbers and characters is not the standard "1=A, 2=B, ..." mapping. You must determine the correct character for each number by solving the crossword.

Your goal is to solve the crossword and recover the original alphabetical characters.

## Flag Construction
Once the crossword has been solved, construct the flag as follows.
Given the following solved crossword:

. . . . F O R M S .
. . . . L . . O . .
. K B X A C . U . .
. I . . G . . S . .
. D . . . S P E A K

Read the alphabetical characters row by row, from left to right.

For each row:
1. Extract all alphabetical characters from left to right.
2. Keep the rows in their original top-to-bottom order.
3. Separate each row using "_".
4. Enclose the resulting string in "kBxAc{...}".

This produces:
FORMS_LO_KBXACU_IGS_DSPEAK

Therefore, the flag is:
kBxAc{FORMS_LO_KBXACU_IGS_DSPEAK}
